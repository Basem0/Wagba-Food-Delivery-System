Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first order.

How fast is delivery?
Most orders arrive in 20–35 minutes. You can watch your driver move on a live map in real time.
Which payment methods are supported?
We support secure card payments via Stripe, with a demo mode for testing. Coupons are applied at checkout.
Can I become a driver?
Yes! Sign up, complete your vehicle profile, get approved by an admin, and start accepting deliveries near you.
How do restaurants join?
Open a restaurant account, add your menu and storefront details, and an admin approves your profile before you go live.
Is there an app?
Wagba runs beautifully in your browser on phone and desktop — no install needed.
